Referral journeys where enabled

Drivest supports role-based referral paths for learners and instructors.

  • Instructor to learner
  • Instructor to instructor
  • Learner to learner
  • Learner to instructor
  • Referral benefits remain conditional and subject to eligibility, verification, anti-fraud review, and reversal rules.

Review and trust signals

Where enabled, instructor trust signals are tied to real Drivest activity rather than anonymous public ratings.

  • Visible instructor reviews are linked to completed Drivest lessons.
  • Instructors can respond to visible reviews.
  • Verification, profile, and review visibility can vary by rollout, moderation state, and readiness.

Booking terms where enabled

Independent instructors deliver the lessons. Where booking features are enabled, fees, payment handling, and cancellation terms are shown before confirmation.

  • Booking-specific terms are shown before confirmation
  • Any fees, payment timing, and cancellation handling are disclosed in the relevant flow
  • Independent instructors remain responsible for the lessons they deliver

Availability and positioning

Instructor pages should stay neutral about local depth and availability unless the product can prove it.

  • Marketplace depth and lesson availability can vary by location and rollout stage
  • Lesson-request flows and payments should be described as where enabled
  • Independent instructors remain responsible for their own lessons, licences, insurance, and legal compliance
