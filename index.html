Built for learners and instructors

Drivest helps future learners start earlier, helps current learners stay clear, and gives independent instructors a cleaner place to handle enquiries and linked-learner context where marketplace features are enabled.

For future learners and learners

Start before lessons, keep theory simple, and move into local practice and driving support at the right stage.

  • Begin theory, signs, Highway Code, and fines preparation early before practical lessons start
  • Choose your stage and preferred language during onboarding
  • Study theory content with mastery flow, topic quizzes, mock tests, and English Peek when needed
  • Request instructor support where learner-facing marketplace features are enabled
  • Review analytics, confidence, resume state, and next-step recommendations

For instructors

Set up instructor information, availability preferences where enabled, and linked learner context without heavy admin.

  • Create a public profile and set availability preferences where listing is enabled
  • Handle lesson requests and booking or payment updates where those flows are enabled
  • Link learners and review progress from Instructor Hub where enabled
  • Work with booking updates, learner context, and dispute records in one place
