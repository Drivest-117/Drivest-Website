See how it works

Driving support changes by stage.

  • Before lessons: future learner drivers can start theory, signs, Highway Code, and fines revision early in a format they can actually understand.
  • Before test day: practical learners need repetition around a selected centre, clearer coaching context, and instructor access.
  • After passing: new drivers need calmer route choice, parking support, and confidence-building guidance.

Safety and trust

  • Real-world driving, lessons, bookings, and navigation must follow UK legal requirements.
  • Drivest guidance is advisory only and may be incomplete or delayed.
  • Practice routes are reconstructed for learning and are not official DVSA routes.
  • Keep full control of the vehicle and always follow live road signs, road markings, and traffic laws.
  • Do not interact with the app while driving.
  • Independent instructors remain responsible for their own lessons, licences, insurance, and legal compliance.

On arrival

Parking outputs are advisory only and may not reflect live restrictions.

Check local signage and restrictions before leaving the vehicle.
