What stays simple

You do not need to buy every module at once. Each plan matches a different stage of the learner-to-driver journey.

Upgrade when needed

Free Learning can stay free until you want centre practice or yearly driving support.

Practice stays focused

Selected-Centre Practice is the only monthly plan and stays tied to one chosen centre while active.

Bundle only for overlap

Annual Bundle is for people who want yearly navigation plus a short route-practice window.

Pricing questions

Short answers before you choose a plan.

What is included in Free Learning?

Free Learning covers theory-first preparation and onboarding, so learners can begin before paying for centre practice or yearly driving support.

How does Selected-Centre Practice work?

It is a monthly plan tied to one chosen test centre, with reconstructed practice routes, off-route alerts, and offline packs while the subscription stays active.

What does the Annual Bundle include?

The Annual Bundle combines 12 months of navigation support with one month of selected-centre practice. Each entitlement keeps its own timeline.

Are practice routes official driving test routes?

No. Practice routes are reconstructed or generated for learning support only and are not official driving test routes.

317 UK test centres are included in Drivest's public coverage dataset above the 2-route threshold. 3,538 reconstructed practice routes are shown across the UK.
