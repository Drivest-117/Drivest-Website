Privacy Policy

Drivest Mobile Application • Version 1.0 • Last updated: 21 December 2025

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Drivest collects, uses, stores, and protects personal data when you use the Drivest mobile application.

Drivest acts as a data controller under the UK General Data Protection Regulation and the Data Protection Act 2018.

2. Company Information

Studio X, Innovation Centre, Boundary Road, Colchester, Essex, CO4 3ZQ

Email: admin@drivest.uk

3. Data We Collect

Data you provide voluntarily:

  • Email address when contacting support or creating an account
  • Optional learning preferences

Data collected automatically:

  • Device type and operating system
  • App version
  • Crash reports and performance diagnostics
  • Anonymous usage statistics

Location data:

  • Used on device for route and learning features
  • Continuous location history is not stored on Drivest servers

We do not collect:

  • Driving licence data
  • Payment card information
  • Biometric data
  • Examiner or DVSA identifiers

4. How We Use Data

  • Provide app functionality
  • Enable learning modules and route practice
  • Track XP and learning progress
  • Improve performance and stability
  • Respond to support and legal enquiries

Personal data is not sold and is not used for third party advertising.

5. Legal Basis for Processing

  • Contract, to provide the app
  • Legitimate interests, app improvement and security
  • Consent, for optional analytics or notifications
  • Legal obligation, where required by law

6. Analytics and Service Providers

Limited third party providers support analytics, crash reporting, and hosting. All operate under UK GDPR compliant agreements.

7. Data Security and Retention

Appropriate technical and organisational measures protect personal data. Data is retained only for as long as necessary.

8. International Transfers

Where applicable, international processing uses UK GDPR approved safeguards.

9. Your Rights

  • Access your data
  • Correct inaccuracies
  • Request deletion
  • Restrict or object to processing
  • Withdraw consent

Requests should be sent to admin@drivest.uk. Identity verification applies.

10. Children

Drivest is intended for users aged 16 and above. Data from children is not knowingly collected.

11. Complaints

Concerns should be raised with Drivest first. You may also complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office.
