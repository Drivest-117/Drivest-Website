Instructor marketplace terms

Marketplace lessons are delivered by independent instructors. Drivest records an 8% platform fee on completed lessons and no commission on cancelled lessons.

  • 48 hours or more before a lesson: full refund
  • 24 to 48 hours before a lesson: up to 25% may be retained by the instructor
  • 12 to 24 hours before a lesson: up to 50% may be retained by the instructor
  • Less than 12 hours before a lesson or no-show: no refund
  • If the instructor cancels, the learner receives a full refund

Safety and trust

  • Real-world driving, lessons, bookings, and navigation must follow UK legal requirements.
  • Drivest guidance is advisory only and may be incomplete or delayed.
  • Practice routes are reconstructed for learning and are not official DVSA routes.
  • Keep full control of the vehicle and always follow live road signs, road markings, and traffic laws.
  • Do not interact with the app while driving.
  • Independent instructors remain responsible for their own lessons, licences, insurance, and legal compliance.
