Learners and instructors start differently. Drivest gives learners a simple path into theory and practice, while instructors get a separate setup for profiles, availability, bookings, and Instructor Hub.

Two clear starting routes

Learners can start in the right language and stage. Instructors can set up a public profile, availability, and learner management without extra admin first.

Getting started

Choose learner preparation or instructor

Drivest onboarding reflects whether you are starting learner preparation, actively learning to drive, or running an independent instructor profile.

Pick your stage and language

Future learners and current learners choose their stage, language, and safety preferences before using theory, practice, or navigation features.

Study, practise, or book lessons

Use learning modules, selected-centre practice routes, instructor discovery, and secure lesson booking from the same platform.

Review progress and next steps

Analytics, readiness signals, booking updates, and confidence history help learners and instructors plan what comes next.

Built for learners and instructors

Drivest helps future learners start earlier, helps current learners stay clear, and gives independent instructors a simpler place to win bookings and manage linked learners.

For future learners and learners

Start before lessons, keep theory simple, and move into local practice and bookings when you are ready.

  • Begin theory, signs, Highway Code, and fines preparation early before practical lessons start
  • Choose your stage and preferred language during onboarding
  • Study theory content with mastery flow, topic quizzes, mock tests, and English Peek when needed
  • Book independent instructors and track lesson status in one app
  • Review analytics, confidence, resume state, and next-step recommendations

For instructors

Get found, publish slots, take lesson requests, and keep learner context together without heavy admin.

  • Create a public profile and publish availability slots
  • Accept lesson requests and monitor booking or payment status
  • Link learners and review progress from Instructor Hub
  • Work with bookings, reviews, and dispute records in one place
