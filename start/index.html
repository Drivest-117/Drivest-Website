How Drivest works

Future learners and learners use the same preparation path, while instructors follow a separate operating flow. Drivest uses role-based onboarding so the right legal prompts, permissions, modules, and progress tools appear in the right order.

Role-based onboarding

The first choice decides what the person sees next. Future learners and learners move into theory and practice support, while instructors move into profile setup, marketplace readiness, and Instructor Hub operations.

Start learner preparation

Use the learner preparation flow whether you are starting theory early or already moving into practical preparation, instructor booking, and calmer navigation.

  • Future learner drivers can begin theory, signs, Highway Code, and fines preparation early before practical lessons start.
  • When ready, create or continue a learner profile, choose your current stage, and confirm the safety and legal acknowledgements used before data collection starts.
  • Pick one of 32 supported languages, then peek into English whenever you want to cross-check the same theory question.
  • Set your analytics, notification, and location preferences before opening Theory, Traffic Signs, Highway Code, or Fines and Penalties.
  • Move into Practice, Find Instructor, and Navigation as your confidence score, booking history, and progress tracking build up.

Join as instructor

Use the instructor flow when you need profile approval, marketplace visibility, linked learners, bookings, and operating records from the start.

  • Create your instructor account and complete the professional legal flow and instructor agreement required before listing.
  • Set up your public profile, coverage area, availability, and lesson preferences so learners can discover and request slots.
  • Open Instructor Hub to link learners, track bookings and payment status, and keep coaching context together in one place.
  • Use reviews, dispute records, notifications, and learner insights to manage your independent instructor business through Drivest.

Getting started

Choose learner preparation or instructor

Drivest onboarding reflects whether you are starting learner preparation, actively learning to drive, or running an independent instructor profile.

Pick your stage and language

Future learners and current learners choose their stage, language, and safety preferences before using theory, practice, or navigation features.

Study, practise, or book lessons

Use learning modules, selected-centre practice routes, instructor discovery, and secure lesson booking from the same platform.

Review progress and next steps

Analytics, readiness signals, booking updates, and confidence history help learners and instructors plan what comes next.

Built for future learners, learners, and instructors

Drivest now supports early theory preparation, the live learner journey, and the instructors behind lessons and bookings.

For future learners and learners

Start preparing before practical lessons begin, then move into practice, instructor booking, secure lesson payments, and calmer navigation after your test.

  • Begin theory, signs, Highway Code, and fines preparation early before practical lessons start
  • Choose your stage and preferred language during onboarding
  • Study theory content and cross-check in English when needed
  • Book independent instructors and track lesson status in one app
  • Review analytics, confidence, and next-step recommendations

For instructors

Run a lighter-weight operating hub while staying an independent instructor business.

  • Create a public profile and publish availability
  • Accept lesson requests and monitor booking or payment status
  • Link learners and review progress from Instructor Hub
  • Work with bookings, reviews, and dispute records in one place
