Learners and instructors start differently. Drivest gives learners a simple path into theory and practice, while instructors get a separate setup for profiles, availability preferences, booking flows where enabled, and Instructor Hub.

Two clear starting routes

Learners can start in the right language and stage. Instructors can set up a public profile, availability, and learner management without extra admin first.

  • Use English Peek whenever you want to compare the same theory question in English.
  • Move from theory into practice routes and instructor search where enabled as your confidence grows.
  • Keep bookings, progress, and next-step guidance in one app.

Getting started

Choose learner preparation or instructor

Drivest onboarding reflects whether you are starting learner preparation, actively learning to drive, or running an independent instructor profile.

Pick your stage and language

Future learners and current learners choose their stage, language, and safety preferences before using theory, practice, or navigation features.

Study, practise, or request lessons

Use learning modules, selected-centre practice routes, instructor discovery, and lesson-request flows from the same platform where enabled.

Review progress and next steps

Analytics, readiness signals, booking updates, and confidence history help learners and instructors plan what comes next.

Built for learners and instructors

Drivest helps future learners start earlier, helps current learners stay clear, and gives independent instructors a cleaner place to handle enquiries and linked-learner context where marketplace features are enabled.

For future learners and learners

Start before lessons, keep theory simple, and move into local practice and driving support at the right stage.

  • Begin theory, signs, Highway Code, and fines preparation early before practical lessons start
  • Choose your stage and preferred language during onboarding
  • Study theory content with mastery flow, topic quizzes, mock tests, and English Peek when needed
  • Request instructor support where learner-facing marketplace features are enabled
  • Review analytics, confidence, resume state, and next-step recommendations

For instructors

Set up instructor information, availability preferences where enabled, and linked learner context without heavy admin.

  • Create a public profile and set availability preferences where listing is enabled
  • Handle lesson requests and booking or payment updates where those flows are enabled
  • Link learners and review progress from Instructor Hub where enabled
  • Work with booking updates, learner context, and dispute records in one place
