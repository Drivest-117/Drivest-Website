# Drivest

> Drivest is an independent UK learner-driver platform for multilingual theory prep, selected-centre practice routes, instructor workflows where enabled, and calmer first drives.

Drivest is a public UK learner-driver platform site. Canonical public content is published at http://127.0.0.1:4173/. Practice routes are reconstructed or generated for learning only and are not official driving test routes. Public coverage in this export includes 317 test centres and 3538 practice routes. Coverage data shown on the site was generated on 19 June 2026.
